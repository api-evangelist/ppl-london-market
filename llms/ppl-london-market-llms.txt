# PPL (Placing Platform Limited)

> The London Market's not-for-profit electronic placing platform. PPL lets brokers and carriers
> quote, negotiate, bind and renew open-market (re)insurance risks electronically across almost
> all classes of business written in London, and is one of the accredited placing platforms
> underpinning Lloyd's Blueprint Two. It is market infrastructure — it writes no insurance and
> carries no risk. The PPL Next Gen API surface is five REST APIs, each with a published
> OpenAPI 3.0.1 document and a Postman collection, built on REST standards and the ACORD GRLC
> data model.

## Access model — read this first

Reading the documentation is open to anyone. CALLING the APIs is market-gated, not self-serve.
There is no API key, no free tier, no public sandbox credential and no signup form. A consumer
must complete three gates:

1. Developer Portal onboarding — email PPLEnquiries@placingplatformlimited.com.
2. Subscribe to LIMOSS API Common Services and be guested into the Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD)
   tenant of the LIMOSS API Gateway. Each environment (Sand, PreProd, Prod) is guested separately.
3. Register an X.509 client certificate — a different one per environment.

Every business call then needs: an Entra ID bearer token whose `scp` claim includes
`user_impersonation`, mutual TLS with the registered certificate, and the `X-Auth-Impersonated-User`
and `X-Auth-Team` headers. Updates additionally need the `X-Last-Modified` concurrency precondition.
Only `/health` answers anonymously.

## APIs

- [PPL Placements API](https://developer.pplnextgen.com/placements): Placements, Programmes,
  Contracts, Sections, carrier participations, role assignment and broker-side negotiation of
  terms. The bind-side surface. 16 paths, 32 operations.
  Base URL: https://api.londonmarketgroup.co.uk/ppl/nextgen/placements/v1
- [PPL Submissions API](https://developer.pplnextgen.com/submissions): underwriters retrieve
  broker submissions and manage their negotiation of terms of cover, including reassignment and
  sending a response back to the broker. The quote-side surface. 8 paths, 10 operations.
  Base URL: https://api.londonmarketgroup.co.uk/ppl/nextgen/submissions/v1
- [PPL Organisations API](https://developer.pplnextgen.com/organisations): broker and carrier
  organisations, team structures, members and roles, market facilities, market underwriters and
  underwriter stamps. Reference data prerequisite to placement work. 7 paths, 7 operations.
  Base URL: https://api.londonmarketgroup.co.uk/ppl/nextgen/organisations/v1
- [PPL Documents API](https://developer.pplnextgen.com/documents): document files and metadata
  across the Placement hierarchy, including the Market Reform Contract, with versioning, content
  retrieval, download and a separate secured content/download path. The contract-issuance surface.
  8 paths, 11 operations.
  Base URL: https://api.londonmarketgroup.co.uk/ppl/nextgen/documents/v1
- [PPL Events API](https://developer.pplnextgen.com/events): notifications about an interested
  party's involvement in the placement process, plus the business transaction log. PULL ONLY —
  there is no webhook, callback or event-push surface anywhere on the platform. 7 paths, 7
  operations.
  Base URL: https://api.londonmarketgroup.co.uk/ppl/nextgen/events/v1

## What PPL does not have

- No claims or FNOL surface. PPL is a placing platform; London Market claims run through separate
  market infrastructure.
- No webhooks, callbacks, AsyncAPI, streaming or event push of any kind.
- No GraphQL and no gRPC.
- No MCP server.
- No SDKs and no CLI.
- No public status page, no SLA, no dated changelog, no deprecation or sunset policy.
- No /.well-known/ documents and no security.txt.
- No published compliance certifications or trust centre, and no vulnerability-disclosure programme.
- No GitHub organisation and no public Postman workspace (the collections are zip downloads).
